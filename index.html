99 Cuts: Slice Puzzle

Half is harder than it looks.

99 Cuts is a puzzle game: drag one line across a shape and cut it into two halves as close to 50/50 as you can. 99 levels, one twist every ten.

Support

Found a bug, lost progress, or have a question? Open an issue and describe what happened, your phone and the game version (shown when you tap the level number ten times): github.com/msagimbekov/99cuts/issues

Remove ads and restore

The one purchase, Remove ads, gives you the bonus life every tenth level without an ad. It is tied to your App Store or Google Play account: on a new phone open Settings → Restore purchase.
