99 Cuts: Slice Puzzle

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

99 Cuts ("the game") is made by Madi Sagimbekov. This page explains what the game stores and sends. There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking of who you are.

On your phone

Your progress (level, lives, streak) and settings (volume) are saved on your phone only.

Hall of Fame (only if you join)

If you finish all 99 levels you can type a name of up to 10 characters. The name, your run time and the date are stored on our server (Supabase, hosted in the EU) and shown to every player in the Hall of Fame. Do not use your real name if you do not want it shown. To have an entry removed, ask through the support page.

Anonymous usage counts

While the game is open it sends a random install number (made on first launch, not linked to you), the level you are on, your platform (iOS or Android) and the game version, so we can see how many people play and where they get stuck. No name, email, location, contacts or advertising ID is included.

Ads

The game shows optional rewarded video ads and a short ad at every tenth level, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EU and UK the game first shows Google's consent form, where you choose what is allowed. You can also limit ad personalisation in your phone's own ad settings. See Google's advertising policy. The Remove ads purchase skips the ad at every tenth level.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold, or ask us to delete a Hall of Fame entry, through the support page. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
